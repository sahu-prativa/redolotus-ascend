The Origin

"A lotus grows in mud —
yet rises pure."

Redolotus was born from darkness. From a phase of life with no direction, no freedom, no voice. Padmini — the founder — chose to rise. The name carries everything: Redolent, a scent that holds memory. Lotus, a flower that blooms against all conditions. Every fragrance created here carries that intention. When you wear Redolotus, you carry that strength.
