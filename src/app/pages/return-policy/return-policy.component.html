01

Overview

At RedoLotus Ascend, we craft every fragrance with the utmost care and quality. Because our products are personal care items (perfumes and fragrances), we maintain a strict but fair return policy to protect both our customers and the integrity of our products.

Please read this policy carefully before placing your order. By purchasing from us, you agree to the terms outlined below.

02

No Exchange Policy

We do not offer exchanges under any circumstances. This includes:

  • Change of mind after purchase
  • Dislike of scent after delivery
  • Preference for a different variant or size
  • Ordering the wrong product by mistake
  • Gifting situations where the recipient prefers a different fragrance
💡

Tip: Try our Discovery Set (2ml vials) before committing to a full bottle — perfect for exploring our fragrance range at a low cost.

03

When We Accept Returns

We accept returns only in the following two situations:

📦

Wrong Product Delivered

If you received a product different from what you ordered (e.g., wrong fragrance, wrong size), you are fully eligible for a return and refund.

⚠️

Damaged Product Delivered

If your order arrived visibly damaged — broken bottle, leaking seal, crushed packaging — you are eligible for a return and full refund.

All other return requests will be declined. We encourage you to review your order carefully before checkout.

04

How to Raise a Return Request

If your order qualifies for a return, follow these steps:

  1. 1
    Contact us within 48 hours of delivery via WhatsApp at +91 63020 04167 or email us at redolotusascend@gmail.com.
  2. 2
    Share the following:
    • Your Order ID / Name
    • Clear photos or a short video of the wrong / damaged product
    • Photo of the outer packaging and shipping label
  3. 3
    Our team will review your request within 2 business days and respond with next steps.
  4. 4
    If approved, we will arrange a free pickup of the product (where applicable) or guide you on how to ship it back.
  5. 5
    Once the return is received and verified, your full refund will be processed within 5–7 business days.
05

Return Conditions

To be eligible for a return, the following conditions must be met:

  • Return request must be raised within 48 hours of delivery
  • Product must be in its original, unused condition (seal intact, not sprayed)
  • Product must be returned in its original packaging (box, cap, wrap)
  • Clear photographic or video proof of the issue must be provided
  • Return request must be communicated through our official channels only
⛔

Returns will be rejected if the product has been used, seal is broken (unless damaged in transit), or if the request is raised after 48 hours of delivery.

06

Refund Process

Once your return is approved and received:

  • Refund will be credited to your original payment method
  • Processing time: 5–7 business days after return verification
  • UPI / Bank Transfer payments: refund via the same account
  • Prepaid orders: refund to the debit/credit card or wallet used
  • COD orders: refund via bank transfer (account details to be shared by customer)

Shipping charges paid (if any) are non-refundable unless the error was on our end.

07

Non-Returnable Items

The following cannot be returned under any circumstances:

  • Products purchased during sale, discount, or promotional events
  • Discovery Sets / Sample vials (2ml)
  • Products with broken seals (unless damaged in transit with proof)
  • Products that have been used or sprayed
  • Gift sets opened by the recipient
  • Products without original packaging or missing accessories
08

Damaged in Transit

We take great care in packaging every order to minimise transit damage. However, if your parcel arrives damaged due to courier mishandling:

  • Do not accept the delivery if the package is visibly tampered or crushed — ask the delivery agent to mark it as refused
  • If you have already accepted it, photograph the damage immediately before opening
  • Report within 48 hours with photos/video evidence
  • We will file a claim with the courier and process your refund once verified
